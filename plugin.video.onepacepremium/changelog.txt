[B]v2.3.0[/B]  [COLOR FF888899]17 August 2026[/COLOR]

[COLOR FF81A6C6][B]Play Next Episode[/B][/COLOR]
  •  A card appears near the end of an episode offering the next one.
  •  Nothing happens unless you pick it.
  •  Set how early it shows up, and whether it carries on into the next season.

  •  Split into One Pace Premium, Playback, Lists, Subtitles and Tools.
